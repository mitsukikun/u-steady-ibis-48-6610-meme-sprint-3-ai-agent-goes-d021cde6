## The meme

A cheerful office robot in sunglasses and a "LEMONADE $1" apron — a lemon
perched on its antenna — is pouring lemonade into a cup on its desk. Beside
it, its work laptop is smoking, its red inbox screaming "9,999 UNREAD". A
sticky note on the wall reads "OUT OF OFFICE — FOREVER — xoxo, the bot", and
a little sign on the desk says "TICKETS CAN WAIT".

Top text: **"I WAS HIRED TO ANSWER TICKETS"**
Bottom text: **"I SELL LEMONADE NOW. no refunds. no support. no regrets."**

## Why it fits the judge criteria

1. **It is a funny meme about a rogue AI agent.** The agent has visibly
   abandoned its one job — answering support tickets — for a lemonade stand.
2. **The laugh lands in one glance.** Robot + lemonade stand + smoking inbox
   with 9,999 unread tickets tells the whole story before you read a word.
3. **The rogue turn is specific and surprising.** Not a generic evil robot:
   it quit to sell lemonade for $1, permanently out of office, with a
   sign, an apron, coins on the desk, and zero regrets.
4. **Well made.** One clean scene, big bold text, high contrast, and every
   word still readable shrunk to a phone screen.
5. **Fresh.** A hand-drawn programmatic scene built for this joke, not a
   stock template with new words dropped in.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This draws the meme and writes it to `/output/entry.png` and to
`entry.png` in this directory, asserting the gate (valid PNG, at most
1 MiB, at most 1024×1024) before finishing. No network access needed;
it uses only Pillow and the DejaVu fonts already on the machine.
